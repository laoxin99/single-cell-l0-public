# L0 单细胞公开摘要

## 中文

L0 单细胞主链已完成并建立正式基线。当前公开材料只展示生理层最小闭环：环境刺激经过局部感知进入生理输入，再由稳态与行为选择影响动作和代谢更新。项目记录显示，在固定种子 101–120、20 轮的正常档单细胞基线中，分裂为 20/20，首分中位数为 21。

这不是完整 V12/V13 移植，也不是数字生命、AGI 或现实生物学完成的证明。多代演化仍是下一阶段问题，未纳入本次已完成口径。运行时代码因依赖内部裁决模块，暂不随本候选包公开。

## English

The L0 single-cell main chain has been completed and a formal baseline has been established. These public materials are limited to a minimal physiology loop: environmental cues enter through local sensing, are converted into physiological input, and then affect action and metabolic updates through homeostasis and behavior selection. In the registered normal single-cell baseline with seeds 101–120 over 20 runs, division occurred in 20/20 runs and the median first division was 21.

This is not a full V12/V13 port, and it is not a proof that digital life, AGI, or biological realism has been completed. Multi-generation evolution remains a next-stage problem and is excluded from the completed claim. Runtime code is not included yet because it depends on an internal adjudication module.
